# The Brain, the Materia, and the Planes

Greg's substance and dimension design, 12 September 2026. His words are quoted
where they carry it better than a summary would.

Four things arrived in one message and they are **one system**, which is the
reason this document exists rather than four checklist sections written
separately:

1. **The brain** — a high-detail organ with a curved CRT bent into it, which is
   an index you open.
2. **The materia** — every substance, with real experiences, entities and costs.
3. **The chip** — a bloody wired tower implanted in the head, which is the
   bridge to the network on the higher planes.
4. **The planes** — the Kabbalistic and Solomonic ladder, which is *how many
   dimensions there are* and what it costs to appeal to each one.

You do not get to the planes except through the brain. You do not get through
the brain without the chip. The chip is in your head because somebody put it
there while you were captured (AP1.3). **The drugs are the only thing you can
reach without permission**, which is exactly why the institution cares about
them.

---

## 1. Naming it

Greg: *"the drug psyconaught angel demon cellout wizard chaos magic (this
obviously needs a name help me)"*.

Five that fit the world, with what each one buys:

| Name | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **WETWIRE** | The brain, the chip and the network in one word, in CellOutz's voice. Reads as a product name a company would stamp on something they put in your skull without asking. Ugly in the right way. |
| **THE MERKABAH** | The Kabbalistic chariot — the vehicle of ascent. Correct for the planes, and it makes the drug index sound like a vehicle rather than a cabinet, which is what it is. Heavier and more occult. |
| **MATERIA** | Plain, old, alchemical. *Materia medica* is the real term for a pharmacopoeia. Understated next to everything else in this game, which is why it might be the one. |
| **THE GREY CHAPEL** | Where you go to meet something. Carries the deliriant register — datura and Benadryl are not recreational and should not sound it. |
| **ASCENT STACK** | Ties it to the wizardsonlyfoolz ladder already in the game, and "stack" is both a software word and what people call a combination of substances. |

**My pick: WETWIRE for the whole system, MATERIA for the drug index inside it.**
That gives you two registers doing different jobs — WETWIRE is what CellOutz
calls the thing in your head, MATERIA is what the thing in your head calls the
cabinet. Two institutions naming the same object differently is already this
game's central rule.

## 2. The brain

> *"the psyconaught thing can be in the brain index which is the brain you have,
> i'm thinking a brain in high detail that has a CRT bent screen showing you the
> inside of the brain, which can be the index for some optional knowledge and the
> drug index"*

- A **real organ**, not an icon — `BaselineHuman` already renders organs and this
  is the most detailed one in the game.
- A **CRT bent into the cortex**, curved the way AH3.2's tutorial sets are
  curved, showing the inside of the brain from inside the brain.
- It is an **index you open**, and most of what is in it is optional. That is the
  point: this is where knowledge lives that the game never requires you to have.
- **Gore and detail are not decoration here.** Greg: *"all the gore bones vfx
  blood detail wants to be visceral"*. The organ is wet. The chip is bolted into
  wet tissue. It should be uncomfortable to look at your own brain, which is the
  correct feeling for a thing somebody installed in you while you were restrained.

### The door in it

> *"in the drugs brain part there obviously a door with a 9g or 8g whatever tower
> in the brain implanted with a bloody wired chip"*

The tower is the **bridge**. Everything about the higher planes goes through it,
which means:

- **The network on the higher planes is reached through your own skull.** The
  Wire (Q) is the network at ground level; this is what the Wire looks like from
  above.
- It is **hardware somebody else installed**, so it can be revoked, traced, or
  used to find you. AK1.2 already says the agency sees what you see.
- Radiation from the towers is what melts you at 8g and 9g (AO1.3). **The thing
  that connects you is the thing that is killing you**, and that is the whole
  bargain of this world.

## 3. The planes, and how many there are

> *"it should be like ancient kabbalah solomonic magic system of the tree of life
> of how many dimensions and planes of stuff… idk how many, if it goes up to like
> 15d"*

The tradition already answers this, and the answer lands almost exactly on
Greg's guess, which is a good sign the instinct was right.

**The ten sephiroth, plus the one that is not on the map.**

| Plane | Sephirah | What it is here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **3D** | Malkuth — the Kingdom | The world. Where the game is played |
| **4D** | Yesod — the Foundation | **The wizard eyes.** Brief, earned, and the only higher plane you see for free |
| **5D** | Hod — Splendour | Structure, language, the Wire seen from above |
| **6D** | Netzach — Victory | Appetite, endurance, what a body wants |
| **7D** | Tiferet — Beauty | The centre. Where entities will actually speak with you |
| **8D** | Gevurah — Severity | Judgement. The verdict on a kill lives here (AJ5) |
| **9D** | Chesed — Mercy | The other half of that, and it disagrees |
| **—** | **Da'ath — Knowledge** | **The Abyss. Not on the map, and it is where the deliriants take you** |
| **10D** | Binah — Understanding | Form, limit, the shape of a thing |
| **11D** | Chokmah — Wisdom | Force without form. Very little survives here |
| **12D** | Keter — the Crown | The godhead is on the other side of this (AQ) |

And the **four worlds** — Assiah, Yetzirah, Beriah, Atziluth — are not more
planes. They are **registers each plane can be seen in**, which is how the same
place looks different on mushrooms than on ketamine. Twelve planes seen through
four registers is where "fifteen or more" comes from, and it gives the art an
enormous amount of room without inventing a single thing.

**Da'ath is the good one.** It is the plane that is real, unmapped, and cannot
be reached deliberately. Deliriants go there. Nothing comes back with anything
useful. This is the honest way to portray datura and diphenhydramine — not as a
trip with better graphics, but as *the one the map does not have*.

### Appealing to a plane

> *"in the kabbalah and ancient solomon esq magic system you have to appeal it to
> the dimensions"*

You do not travel up. **You petition, and you are admitted or you are not.** The
Solomonic form is the right one: a name, a seal, an offering, and a licence to
depart. This connects straight to AJ — a sigil is how you ask, and the gods of
AJ3 are who you are asking.

## 4. The materia

Every category, because Greg asked for every category.

| Class | In the game |
| --- | --- |
| **Stimulant, legal** | Coffee, tea, energy drinks by brand, Zyns, cigarettes, vapes |
| **Stimulant** | Cocaine, amphetamine, the cathinones |
| **Cannabinoid** | Joints, spliffs, blunts, bongs, strains that differ |
| **Psychedelic** | LSD, mushrooms by species and strain, mescaline, the 2C family, the DOx family |
| **Dissociative** | Ketamine, PCP, DXM, the arylcyclohexylamines |
| **Deliriant** | Datura, nutmeg, diphenhydramine. **These are not fun and must not read as fun** |
| **Empathogen** | MDMA and its relatives |
| **Depressant** | Benzodiazepines, alcohol, the Z-drugs |
| **Opioid** | The whole ladder, with the whole cost |
| **Research chemical** | The long tail, as easter eggs |
| **New-world** | Substances that only exist after the collapse, made from what is left |

### The rules that make it a mechanic rather than a menu

- **Everything costs.** Greg: *"if you want to experiment with drugs and ketamine
  it does cost you too"*. Body, standing, time, and attention — the godhead
  notices a charged sigil and it notices this (AQ's `DRAWS`).
- **Tolerance and comedown are real and tracked.** The second time is not the
  first time. `world_clock.gd` makes that measurable.
- **Set and setting.** The same substance in a safe room and in a tunnel are not
  the same experience, because the entities you meet come from where you are.
- **Strains differ.** Two mushrooms are not one item with a number.
- **A drug is an object** — a baggie, a blister, a tab, a bag of grass with a
  weight — carried in CARRY, priced by the Choir, stolen off a body.
- **Deliriants are horror.** Full stop. They go to Da'ath, the entities there are
  not interested in you, and you come back having lost time.

### The boundary, stated once

This game portrays **experiences, entities, consequences and costs** — the
register of a trip report, which is what Erowid and PsychonautWiki actually are
as *writing*. What it does not carry, in the fiction or in the files, is
**dosage tables, routes of administration, preparation, or combination advice**.
That line costs the game nothing: nobody has ever found a trip report less
frightening for omitting the milligrams, and the horror of datura is entirely in
the account and not at all in the quantity. Everything Greg is reaching for —
maturity, realism, the entities, the cost — lives on the right side of it.

## 5. The trips

> *"the world is filled with demonic and jesterish entities and then the game
> would turn full hellscape or angelscape DMT-esque psychedelic trip in cutscenes
> that are all optional"*

- **Optional, always.** A player who never takes anything sees the whole game.
- **Entities are consistent.** The same thing met twice is the same thing, and it
  remembers. They go in `WorldHistory` like everyone else.
- **Jesterish is the house register** — the jester is already on the back of the
  handheld, and it is the right face for something that is not hostile and is
  not on your side.
- **Hellscape and angelscape are the same place in two registers** (the four
  worlds above), not two sets of assets.
- **The flipped internet**: the Wire seen from 5D, where the posts are being made
  by something else.
- All of it runs on Agent B's `psychedelic.gdshader` with different dials
  (`DESIGN/FINAL_V.md` §16) rather than a bespoke system per substance.

## 5b. Altitude is the gate

Greg: *"the higher you have to be to talk or even fight, conjure, evoke etc"*.

**This is the mechanic the whole section was missing.** Substances are not a
side activity with visual effects — they are *how you reach the thing you want
to reach*, and each plane has a floor you have to be above to do anything there
at all.

| What you want to do | What it needs |
| --- | --- |
| **See** a plane | Enough to perceive it. The wizard eyes (4D) are free and brief; everything above is bought |
| **Talk** to something on it | Higher than seeing, and sustained — a conversation is not a glimpse |
| **Conjure or evoke** | Higher again, plus the sigil (AJ), plus the name |
| **Fight** something there | The highest, because you have to be as present as it is |

Three consequences fall straight out of that and all three are good:

- **Coming down mid-conversation is a real failure state.** The entity does not
  wait, and it remembers being left.
- **The substance decides which plane you reach**, not a menu. A dissociative and
  a psychedelic at the same intensity do not open the same door.
- **You cannot fight the godhead sober**, and that is not a difficulty setting.
  It is why it summons you (AQ1.3) rather than letting you arrive.

## 5c. They remember you

Greg: *"build relationships too, they are more mysterious, voice distorted"*.

Entities are **subjects in `WorldHistory`** like every person in the game, which
means every system already built applies to them: they have a record, they can
be wronged, and they can be wrong about you.

- **A relationship accumulates across trips.** The seventh time you meet
  something is not the first time.
- **Voice is distorted and gets clearer with standing.** This is the best
  possible readout of a relationship: at nothing you catch one word in five, and
  what an entity is willing to be understood saying is the whole mechanic. No
  meter needed.
- **They are mysterious because they withhold, not because they are vague.** An
  entity that knows something and will not say it yet is interesting; one that
  speaks in fog is not.
- **They can be owed, and they collect.** AR2.4's contracts are the same system.
- **They disagree with each other**, the way the gods do about a kill (AJ5).

## 5d. The register

Greg: *"like a Postal 2 meets Schedule 1 meme, but then serious edgy political
commentary — you can also lace people"*.

That is a real tonal instruction and it resolves a tension this project has been
carrying: the game is funny *and* it is serious, and the way those coexist is
that **the joke is never at the expense of a person and always at the expense of
an institution.**

- **Postal 2's contribution** is deadpan bureaucracy while the world is on fire —
  a man doing paperwork about a body while the body waits. The interstitial
  already talks like this.
- **Schedule 1's contribution** is that the economy is the gameplay: production,
  cutting, distribution, territory, and everybody's cut.
- **The commentary is the target, and the target is institutional.** The bank
  that writes liens against organs, the agency that owns the sky, a godhead that
  enslaves through professional development.

**Lacing** is the sharp edge of it and it belongs, with its consequences
attached:

- You can put something in something somebody else is going to take.
- **The game does not congratulate you.** It records it, the way it records
  everything.
- `witness_ledger.gd` already decides who saw what. AE1.4's law responds to what
  was witnessed. AJ5's gods give a verdict and disagree.
- It is a crime with a victim, so the world treats it as one — which is the only
  way a mechanic like this is worth having rather than being edgy for its own
  sake.

## 6. Commissioning your friends

Greg: *"help me make subsection lists on top of the checklist that show me how i
can commission and call all my artist friends to commission them at using their
assets for textures and certain things can be used and built in the trip"*.

The planes are the right place for this, and here is why it actually works as a
commission: **each plane is a brief.** You are not asking somebody for "some
psychedelic art" — you are asking for *Gevurah, seen from Yetzirah*, at a stated
size, for a stated use, with everything else in the game as reference. That is a
brief an artist can price and deliver, and it is a much better one than most
freelance work they will be offered.

Section **AW** in `CHECKLIST.md` is the process. The short version:

1. **One plane, one artist, one brief.** Twelve planes is twelve commissions and
   they do not depend on each other.
2. **Write the brief before you ask.** Plane, register, size, format, what it is
   used for, what it sits next to.
3. **Agree the licence in writing before money moves.** What you may use it for,
   whether you may modify it, whether it survives you selling the game. Get this
   wrong and a shipped game has to be rebuilt.
4. **Credit in the game, not just the readme.** The world index already has a
   cast page.
5. **Pay properly.** These are friends, which is a reason to be *more* careful
   about the paperwork, not less.
6. **Take source, not just exports.** A PSD you can re-export at another size is
   worth several times a flat PNG in two years.
7. **Their work goes in as texture and material**, inside the procedural system
   rather than replacing it — the same rule A v10 sets for Greg's own collage.
