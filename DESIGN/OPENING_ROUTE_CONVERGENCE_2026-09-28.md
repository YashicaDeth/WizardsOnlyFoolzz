# Opening Route Convergence

This is the anti-overwhelm rule for minutes 0–30. The game has many systems;
the opening earns them one at a time instead of listing them.

## One shared tutorial spine

1. **Rebirth and intake** — wake in the vat, understand the body, complete or
   interrupt intake without a hard lock.
2. **Black Mirror acquisition** — every route gives the player the canonical
   handheld. It opens the real menu and makes INDEX, MAP and carried state
   available as the fiction permits.
3. **Route divergence** — elevator, service arcade/drains and recapture/derby
   may use different spaces and tools, but none becomes a different tutorial.
4. **First weapon** — the pickup opens the matching combat and weapon material
   in the Brain Index.
5. **Immediate practice** — the route presents a small readable problem that
   requires the newly revealed verb. A prompt without required play is not a
   tutorial.
6. **Escalated combination** — movement, attack, defence/evasion and one
   route-specific interaction are combined once before the exit.
7. **Distinct surface handoff** — each successful route reaches useful open
   ground in its authored part of the world, with the same core player state.

The access card, breach tool, drains climb, vehicle and other props are allowed
to be route-specific. The Black Mirror and the core learning outcomes are not.

## The build–play–fix loop

Work on one golden route until it is enjoyable from wake-up to surface:

1. Make one small build whose purpose can be stated in one sentence.
2. Play it with DualSense, then keyboard and mouse.
3. Record only the three largest points of confusion, friction or boredom.
4. Fix those before adding another opening mechanic.
5. Keep the route test and one real-input regression that proves the beat.
6. Produce the next build and repeat.

The service-arcade/drains path is the initial golden route because it already
contains the densest chain of physical interactions. Elevator and recapture
routes are reconciled against its proven learning spine afterward; they are not
developed in parallel feature piles.

## Definition of done for one teaching beat

A beat is finished only when all are true:

- the player reaches it through ordinary play;
- the affordance is visible and readable at supported resolutions;
- controller and keyboard/mouse both perform the same action;
- the player must use the verb in a safe, authored problem;
- the game gives immediate visual, physical or audio confirmation;
- the result survives save/load or route handoff when relevant;
- a focused regression test protects the behavior;
- the full route still reaches its surface exit.
