Works order · v0.1 "Escape"4 Oct 2026

Build Plan

The game plan holds every idea. This page holds only what gets built next, so there is an actual game that works: about 20 minutes, from pressing START to stepping out on the surface, on a pad or keyboard. Everything else is switched off for this build, not deleted. Parts come from free low-poly libraries on the internet wherever they exist, so nobody hand-builds what someone already made.

01What v0.1 is

In this build
  • Menu, intro cutscene, short torture load-in
  • Wake in the vat in real red liquid; quick intake (preset plus body and face)
  • Brain screen: pick 2 of 2 working disciplines
  • Breakout: tear cords, break glass, liquid floods out
  • One facility map: vat floor, examiner office, service arcade, Lower Works
  • Heated cleaver, shock staff, pistol; gore, sever, ragdoll on death
  • Examiner boss, his keycard, the staff door
  • A real elevator ride up; arrival on the surface ends the build
  • Death respawns you in the vat with your bloodstain where you fell
  • Simple inventory, one objective line, pause and settings, checkpoint saves
Switched off until v0.2+
  • The other four disciplines, chip heat, brain skill board
  • The Wire, the Board, the pyramid, territories, money
  • Derby, drains route, doctor-chase route
  • Drugs, cases and skins, voice NPCs, planes, Hornee
  • Support Unit and bingyangers (they come back in v0.2)
  • Text inspections, breach-tool puzzle, wall scan, fuse hunt, wound minigame

Why two disciplines: Brawler (heated cleaver and shock staff, heavy directional swings) and Marksman (pistol with aim down sights and VATS). They cover melee and guns with code that mostly exists already. The other four come one at a time once these two feel good.

02The big fix: real rigged bodies

Most of what looks broken (no head, huge shoulders, floating arms, limbs coming apart in ragdolls) comes from the bodies being assembled from loose procedural pieces with no skeleton. The fix is to use a proper rigged low-poly humanoid for every person and keep our anatomy system underneath it: wounds, organs and severing stay ours, they just ride on real bones.

  • Body: a free CC0 rigged base character (Quaternius base characters, or a Mixamo-compatible low-poly human), with our PS1-style shader on top.
  • Animation: Mixamo (free to use in games) and Quaternius animation sets for walk, run, attacks, hit reactions, deaths.
  • Ragdolls: Godot's physical bones on that skeleton, with Jolt physics (built into Godot 4.4+). Tried in the mechanics lab first.
  • Gore: each anatomy zone maps to a bone, so cuts, organs and severing follow the real skeleton.

Rules for every job
  1. Wire before you build. The repo has about 236 systems and many are finished but called by nothing. Before writing a new file, search for the one that already does it (systems/, scene scripts, tests/). Rebuilding an existing system is a failed job.
  2. Free parts from the internet first. CC0 from Quaternius, Kenney, Poly Pizza, ambientCG, Poly Haven; free-for-games from Mixamo; CC-BY from Sketchfab or Freesound with credit. Every imported file is listed in CREDITS.md (source URL, author, licence). Never rip assets from commercial games. Generated art comes in only through tools/import_generated_asset.py.
  3. Pad first, keyboard equal. Every action goes through real InputMap actions with joypad events (systems/input_profile.gd, Souls base layout: R1 light, R2 heavy, L1 block, L2 parry or aim, R2 fire with a gun). On-screen hints come from systems/key_hints.gd and follow the last device used.
  4. One game. Work in your own worktree off claude/launch-latest; run --headless --import once before anything else. Merge happens through the hub gate. Commit small and often so a usage limit cannot lose work.
  5. Look at it. A change that affects what the player sees is not done until you rendered it (capture harness under game/tests/*_capture.tscn, or one windowed run on the right monitor) and opened the PNG. Say which PNGs in the commit message.
  6. Performance. 144 fps or better on Greg's RTX 2060 SUPER in every room you touch (F10 counter or game/tests/perf / frame-budget tests).
  7. Report back in three parts: what is done (with PNG paths and tests run), what you could not verify, and any pre-existing failures you met.
Job 1: Mechanics lab

GOAL. Greg: "a demo dev test area you can just walk around… here's how the liquid physics, here's how the weather works, how the weather interacts with the area, the rain, the storms, acid rain, how the destruction physics works with weapons… then we apply that to the actual full game." EXISTS (use, do not rebuild): systems/world_weather.gd, systems/storm_weather.gd, systems/blood_pool.gd, systems/blood_flow.gd, systems/ground_hole.gd, systems/breakable_prop.gd, systems/world_break.gd, systems/breakable_door.gd, systems/vat_smash.gd, systems/ragdoll_body.gd, systems/baseline_human.gd, systems/penetration.gd, systems/world_xray.gd. BUILD. A 40 x 40 m greybox yard with a covered half and an open half (so weather can be compared under cover and in the open), lit so all five stations read at once. A station is a plinth with a short label and one pad button:

  1. Liquid. A glass tank of red fluid (shader surface with depth tint and wobble). Break the glass: GPU particles with collision pour out, a pooling decal spreads on the floor, splashes when walked through or shot.
  2. Weather. One button steps clear, rain, storm, acid rain. Wet surfaces darken and shine, puddles form in low spots, lightning flashes, cloth and foliage take wind. Acid rain visibly etches a metal prop over 30 s and hurts a dummy standing in the open but not one under cover.
  3. Destruction. A wall, crates, glass panes, a pipe run and open ground. Every v0.1 weapon from job 3 breaks them in authored stages with debris; ground takes a hole sized by the round. Pre-fractured pieces made with Blender's Cell Fracture add-on are swapped in on the hit.

  5. Vehicle pad. An empty marked area for later driving work.

Free-walk with the normal player controller; Start button opens a station menu that teleports you to each plinth.

PROOF. One PNG per station in its "after" state, plus one of the whole yard. TESTS. New tests/mechanics_lab_test.tscn: every station loads and its system answers one input. Existing weather, ground-hole, breakable and vat-smash tests stay green. OPEN. Which weather states the real map uses at launch. Whether acid rain damages the player at full strength. Do not decide; build the dials. OUT. Story, enemies that fight back, the derby itself. Skills. create-game-vfx, godot-shaders, godot-physics.
