# Escape routes out of the facility

Greg's direction, 24 September 2026. Decisions are his; **Assistant proposals**
are not.

Earlier the same day Greg chose "just the two built ones" for the ways out:
the heat elevator, and the derby (shelved as old drain tunnels that surface
you elsewhere). He has since added a third, **chasing the doctor**, and
described the derby route in full.

## Route: chasing the doctor

> "make the third route trying to chase the doctor you can go back and break
> down the door with a weapon which shows the destruction physics which we
> need to implement asap into the game"

> "you can get him but you realise hes a hologram and then he projects you a
> 3d call of himself which is getting the elvator out and exiting the
> building on the top on a helicopter so you see this in a 3d touchdesigner
> deisgned animation that pops up after you question fight and or try and
> kill his hologram which you think is real and then you can gain special
> information and a task goal and possibly a weapon or cybernetic to call
> people which then opens up in the brain index calling and summoning or
> trying to summon different npcs like inviting npocs in the sims on the
> phone"

Decided:

- **The door** is behind the player's vat: the examination room the doctor
  watched from, through glass, at the near end of the Growing Floor. The
  player goes back past their broken vat to reach it.
- **What breaks it:** the restraint, Hollis's gun, a heavy tool near the door,
  or the player's own body. Any of them works.
- **Destruction physics are wanted as soon as possible**, starting with this
  door and then everything the player would hit in the facility: vat glass,
  the gate, props. Real pieces that fly, stay and can be kicked.
- **The chase** leads up a service corridor to his vehicle bay.
- **He is a hologram.** The player reaches him and thinks he's real. Questioning
  him, fighting him or trying to kill him reveals the hologram.
- **Then he calls.** A 3D call of himself pops up: he is taking the elevator
  up and leaving from the roof by helicopter. It is to be a TouchDesigner-
  designed 3D animation.
- **What the player gains:** special information, a task goal, and possibly a
  weapon or a **cybernetic to call people**. That opens calling and summoning
  in the Brain Index: phoning NPCs to invite or summon them, or trying to,
  like inviting people over on the phone in The Sims.

## Route: the derby and the tunnels

> "the derby and car route once you crash you can exit the car and or drive
> it through a tunnel system that gate opens up which then u can drive in
> like its gta tunnels and then you can drive to a drain exit at a dried up
> waterfall thats covered with blood and gore rivers of blood and sestruction
> of trees"

Decided:

- After you crash in the derby, you can **get out of the car, or keep
  driving**.
- **A gate opens onto a tunnel system** you can drive into, like GTA's
  tunnels.
- The tunnels lead to a **drain exit at a dried-up waterfall**, covered in
  blood and gore, with **rivers of blood and destroyed trees**.

## Assistant proposals, not confirmed

- **The hologram is placeholder until the TouchDesigner piece exists.** The
  3D call is built in-engine now (a hologram-shaded miniature of the elevator
  and the helicopter lift-off) behind one call, so Greg's TouchDesigner
  animation can replace it without touching the encounter.
- **The vehicle bay is where route 3 leaves the facility.** After the call,
  the bay's ramp is the way up to the surface, landing the player somewhere
  different from the other routes, as the spine requires ("every exit lands in
  the same persistent world at a different place").
- **The task goal is the roof.** The call tells the player where he went: the
  roof, by helicopter. That becomes the task, and it points at the doctor's
  history and vehicle, which are still open questions.
- **The calling cybernetic is the hologram's own link.** The emitter that
  projected him is a CellOutz call implant; taking it is how the player
  learns to call people.
- **The waterfall exit is built as its own scene**, so the tunnel route on
  Greg's PC branch can drive into it without the two colliding.

## Still open

- The doctor's name, history and vehicle. (The helicopter answers "how he
  leaves"; it doesn't name him.)
- Whether the helicopter, the roof and the doctor can be reached later in
  the game.
- Who can be called and summoned first, and what an NPC who refuses says.
- Whether the heat elevator stays as a fourth way out now that the doctor
  takes an elevator too.
