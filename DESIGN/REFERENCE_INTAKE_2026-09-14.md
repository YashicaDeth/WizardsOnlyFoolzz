# Reference intake — geometry, signal and unreliable revelation

This note records the latest visual reference drop as **art direction**, not a
bundle of factual claims.  The useful game feeling is a person trying to make a
coherent cosmology from too many feeds: sacred diagrams, satellite views,
anatomy scans, product ads, historical ephemera, jokes and frightening certainty
all arriving at once.

## Editorial rule

Every in-world assertion receives a provenance readout: **OBSERVED**,
**ATTRIBUTED**, **CONTESTED**, or **FABRICATED**.  A confident graphic is never
evidence by itself.  The player can compare records, witnesses and physical
traces, and earns the right to say "I do not know" rather than being rewarded
for accepting the loudest story.

Satire has a target: fictional institutions, algorithms, cults, corporate media
and their incentives.  It never turns a real ethnicity, faith or living cultural
tradition into a hidden enemy.  Symbols borrowed from a real tradition need
specific research and context; the game's *Inverted Lattice* and resonance
systems are original fiction rather than claims about that tradition or reality.

## Design primitives worth carrying forward

### The living technical body

- Bodies are partly anatomical, partly circuit diagram: vascular traces,
  nervous-system node maps, transparent organs and signal scars.
- The human/demon boundary is read through masks, grafts, asymmetry and
  behaviour, not caricature or a marker associated with a real group.
- X-ray and organ views are evidence tools: they reveal damage, implants,
  contamination and possibly misleading readings — never medical diagnosis.

### Geometry that acts rather than explains

- Cubes, tetrahedra, octahedra, torus loops, concentric fields and line-net
  diagrams become original *resonance solids*.
- A solid is a readable game object: align it, rotate it, place it in a ritual
  circuit, or use it to stabilize a field.  Its appeal is visual/mathematical,
  not proof of a cosmic theory.
- Render the structures as animated line fields, wireframe overlays and
  procedural After-Effects-like transforms rather than lifted diagrams.

### The sky, the map and the black mirror

- Satellite mode begins as a credible survey tool, then exposes anomalies,
  competing annotations and a confidence score.  The map records both what the
  sensor saw and what its owner published.
- CRT/phone feeds, scrolling ads, warning cards and distorted broadcasts sell
  a narrative before they establish its truth.  Feed density is a horror effect.
- The owner of the satellite system is a fictional agency.  Its surveillance
  and map manipulation are a relationship mechanic, not an allegation about a
  real agency or community.

### Practice, ritual and substances

- Meditation/training provides the reliable, skill-based way to tune a Field
  readout.  Rituals produce persistent permissions only after costs, witnesses
  and consequences are resolved.
- Substances can create temporary, unstable access to a view; they cannot be a
  required "hero dose" or a simple power-up.  Use is portrayed with impairment,
  risk, uncertainty and social consequences, not as real-world advice.
- A player should be able to retreat, sober up and use grounded tools.  The
  fiction rewards care, preparation and relationships over escalation.

### The samsara / consequence wheel

- A three-dimensional karmic wheel appears at major consequence checkpoints.
  Its segments are people and relationships, not moral points.
- The readout asks a concrete question: *did this action free somebody, bind
  them further, or merely move suffering out of frame?*  It displays evidence
  and unresolved harm, not divine authority.

### Reincarnation, mortality and the layers

- Street, Wire, Field and Inverted Lattice are one fictional world-state system.
  The same location changes meaning across layers; an NPC can be alive, absent,
  remembered, copied or transformed without treating this as a statement about
  real death or religion.
- Reincarnation is a narrative and memory mechanic: histories persist and
  witnesses disagree.  "People who cannot die" is a horror premise with rules,
  not a lore excuse to erase consequences.

## Reference register

The archive's strongest recurring visual language is:

- body-in-a-vessel silhouettes; coloured node ladders; circuitry and aura fields;
- sacred-geometry posters, Platonic-solid diagrams and radial flower patterns;
- Saturn storms, black-cube imagery, satellite/survey screens and hostile map
  overlays;
- occult retail, crystal grids, ritual app onboarding and parody wellness ads;
- doomscrolling walls of claims, handwritten pamphlets, maps of influence and
  scrolling misinformation; and
- absurdist release valves: gym culture, cartoon faces, mundane smoke breaks and
  meme timing.

The visual stack should feel deliberately excessive.  The game itself is the
editor: it turns the stack into choices, consequences and checkable evidence.

## Fact boundary for future writing

- Saturn's north-pole hexagon is a real atmospheric jet-stream pattern, useful
  as a visual starting point but not evidence for an occult system.
- MKULTRA was a real historical CIA behavioral-research program.  Its existence
  does not establish broad claims that arbitrary symbols, media, drugs or
  communities form one coordinated plot.
- Recurring circles, cubes, stars and proportional patterns are visually potent
  and widely reused.  In this game, resemblance is a lead to investigate, not a
  verdict.

## First build slice

**Resonance Readout v1** connects systems already in the project:

1. Meditation adds stable Field attunement over time.
2. A ritual can create a persistent, named access condition.
3. A substance effect can temporarily distort or widen a readout, with explicit
   instability and aftermath.
4. Satellite/phone posts carry provenance and confidence, so a player can pin,
   challenge or corroborate them.
5. Major choices update a small 3D consequence wheel tied to named NPCs.

This is deliberately a small vertical slice.  It makes the reference language
playable before we make a larger cosmology diagram or a thousand lore posts.
