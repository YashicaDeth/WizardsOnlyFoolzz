Catalogue Nº1 — the object itself

Allusions to Grandeur

A dense, violent, surreal living-world sandbox. The world existed before you and it remembers what you do to it.

You wake submerged in a vat on the Growing Floor, umbilicals still attached, and you are decanted onto wet grating already injured. There is a debt against your name. The debt is in the meat. You clear it by winning a demolition heat you were never asked to consent to.

Bodies remember here. Injuries, prosthetics and scars persist on every driver in the pit and change how they fight you the next time — not because a writer planned the rematch, but because the simulation kept the receipt.
