What this is

CellOutz owns you. This is the other end of the same axis.

Every subject in Allusions to Grandeur sits somewhere on one line between two poles. CellOutz — Ownership — is the branding on the handheld in your hand from the first scene. WizardsOnlyFoolz is the far end of it: Frequency, the ascending collective, reached the long way rather than bought.

Nothing here is sold. There is no shop, no standing order, no SKU. What lives on this page is the community side of the project — where people who are actually playing it talk to the people building it.

Two sites, one Tree

celloutz.xyz is the storefront: the catalogue, the merch, the pre-order. wizardsonlyfoolz.net (soon) is the commons: the Discord, the bug codex, the people. The switch above always goes to the other one — that is the whole feature.
