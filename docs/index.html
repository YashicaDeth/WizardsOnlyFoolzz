Playable demo · Windows

You wake up in a vat. Somebody owns you. Get out.

Wizards Only Fools is a first-person body-horror escape and survival game by Greg. The demo is the first thirty minutes: you get grown, examined, and filed in a CellOutz tank, then you tear out your own wires and fight or talk your way up through the facility to the surface.

Every build here is a playtest build. Things will break. Tell us when they do: the board below goes straight to the people building it.

Download the demo Latest build · checking…
  1. Unzip the download (right-click → Extract All).
  2. Double-click WizardsOnlyFools.exe.
  3. If Windows warns you: More info → Run anyway. The build is not code-signed yet.
WASD to move, mouse to look, E to use. The body-cam HUD shows every other key next to the thing it works on.

What's in the demo

the first thirty minutes
  1. The intakeThe examiner reads you your file, one receipt at a time. Your answers are who you become.
  2. The wiresEND ALL SUFFERING. Tear the umbilicals out yourself, then GET REVENGE and break the glass.
  3. The Growing FloorOther tanks, other subjects. One of them is wearing clothes you could use.
  4. Hollis and the doorA guard, a gun and a biometric lock that wants his hand. Coerce him or put him down.
  5. Death is a vatDie and you are regrown in whoever owns you now. Your gear stays on the old body.
  6. Two ways upRide the heat elevator past the sentinel, or crawl the old drains with something that hunts by sound.
  7. The surfaceCome out over the Hunt: the overworld, its settlements, and everything that wants your blood.
