# Wizards Only Fools

A first-person biopunk occult horror game in Godot. You are a **decanted clone owned by debt**, woken early in a CellOutz vat on a prison planet. Your soul hacks the chip in your skull, you tear yourself out, get revenge, escape the facility, and then you **fight God to make Him look at you**. This page is the whole game in one place: what it is, every system, and the order it gets built in.

## IThe game

Earth is an industry and prison planet in a **perpetual post-apocalypse**. The nuclear war is fake; an alien apocalypse took the planet and the government still lives under it, eating it. The game never confirms which story is true. You are a **captured spirit**: not powerful, unkillable. Death regrows you in the vat of whoever owns your body, and the world does not rewind.

The first 30 minutes are the **facility escape**, a subtle tutorial. The next 30 are the Ashbloom Expanse above it, still teaching quietly, then opening into free exploration. It is a save game with a main story, an ending and a post-game. Not a roguelike.

#### Tone

Postal 2 deadpan meets Schedule 1 economy, with real political bite. The joke is always on an institution, never on a person. Horror first: Outlast, Alien Isolation, old Silent Hill, Cruelty Squad. Crunchy low-poly, grainy, modern lighting.

#### Combat lineage

Dark Souls 1–3 and Bloodborne for structure and bosses, Mordhau and Half Sword for weight, Doom for speed, Sniper Elite for the X-ray kill, Fallout for VATS, Nioh for stances. Controller first.

#### World lineage

Fallout 3 and New Vegas, Kenshi bodies, Shadow of Mordor nemesis, Far Cry outposts, RDR2 and GTA random events. No bigger than Fallout 4.

#### Art rule

Greg's own art is the foundation. Generated images guide models, textures and effects; they don't ship as final art. Palette: near-black, dried blood, arterial red, copper, bone, acid green.

## IICosmology: as above, so below

Below · Descent
#### CellOutz

The demons who got out of the cell. A corporation that sells you your own organs back. The HUD, the phone, the bounty platform and the liability notices are all its products. You have carried hell's merchandise since the first scene.

The waist · Limbo
#### You

A CellOut wizard: half demon, half angel. Your alignment is one number between the two ends, starting at zero. You want freedom from rebirth and from the Godhead, and to force a hearing so the earth can be fixed.

Above · Ascent
#### wizardsonlyfoolz

The mage collective: higher-frequency entities, gods, the angelic register. A guild that won't take you seriously. Angels are proud, not automatically good. The game is named after the club you're locked out of.

### Above it all

- **The Godhead** is not revealed. It taunts, it teaches, and its teaching is how it gets its hold. It summons you; you don't travel to it. The final fight is not a damage race.

- **The gods are what is actually worshipped**: markets, metrics, engagement, brands, each a real entity whose attention you can attract. A god for each planet, the moon and the sun, visible at their hours through a broken firmament.

- **Samsara wheel**: on a permanent death a small wheel rises and named powers argue whether the soul was freed or bound again. The game never rules.

- **Quantum immortality**: the universe restarts and you don't. What carries is scars, not statistics.

The Tree of Life's ten sephiroth, plus the one that isn't on the map. You don't travel up; you **petition**, with a name, a seal, an offering and a licence to depart, and you are admitted or you are not. You reach the planes only through the brain, and the brain only through the chip.

 | 5D | Hod · Splendour | Structure and language; the Wire seen from above, posts made by something else |

 —Da'ath · Knowledge**The Abyss.** Not on the map, can't be reached on purpose. Deliriants take you here and nothing comes back useful
 | 10D | Binah · Understanding | Form and limit, the shape of a thing |

The **four worlds** (Assiah, Yetzirah, Beriah, Atziluth) are registers each plane can be seen in, so one place looks different on mushrooms than on ketamine. Twelve planes through four registers is where "fifteen-plus dimensions" comes from.

### Altitude is the gate

 | To | You need |

 | See | Enough to perceive it. Wizard eyes are free and brief; everything above is bought |

 | Talk | Higher and sustained. Coming down mid-conversation is a real failure, and the entity remembers being left |

 | Conjure / evoke | Higher again, plus the sigil and the name |

Entities are people in the world history: they remember you, can be wronged, and their distorted voices get clearer as your standing grows. Each plane is also a commission brief for a friend's art: one plane, one artist, one brief.

#### Signal territory

Feeds, forums, masts, presses. Taken by out-publishing, discrediting, hijacking, flooding or cutting the mast. It controls what the world believes, and grudges spread through it.

#### Ground territory

Camps, towns, yards. Raided for things at a moment you choose, scouted against real schedules. Liberate, help the occupier (corruption), or arm the townspeople. Burned places stay burned; desolated land slowly recovers.

#### Crew

People join through history: bonds, shared enemies, debts, defections, hire or coercion. They can refuse, die permanently and betray.

#### The pyramid

A nemesis hierarchy of two halves, ascent above and corruption below, you at the waist. Power at both tips, the crowd in the middle. Spin, zoom and fly into it in the World Index.

## VThe body, the brain and the materia

#### One anatomical rig

Every person, demon and alien is the same body with different parameters, opened in the same detail. Skin, fat, muscle, bone, organs and cybernetics as layers; wounds recorded against zones; severed limbs you can pick up and swing.

#### The vat and the intake

Character creation is the examination. Four routes: preset, random, birth chart, personality instrument. Races: Decanted, Soft Rot, Marrow-Cut, Roadborn, Unreset, Lantern-Born. Traits with a point budget, and CLERICAL ERROR: part of your sheet is wrong and you aren't told which.

#### WETWIRE

Your brain, opened: a wet organ with a CRT bent into the cortex and a bloody wired chip bolted in while you were restrained. It is the index you open, the skill board and the bridge to the planes. The thing that connects you is the thing that is killing you.

#### MATERIA

Every substance class, each with real experiences, entities, tolerance and comedown on the world clock. Set and setting matter. Deliriants are horror and lead to Da'ath. No dosage or preparation detail anywhere. Lacing is possible, and the world records it as a crime.

#### Death

You regrow in the vat of whoever claims you. Your gear stays at your corpse, and your blood and soul fragments drop as a pulsing organ-sigil you hear before you see. Die again before you reach it and they're gone. The character preset is kept.

#### Freed vat subjects

Vat madness: subjects held and tortured in the facility, and the ones you free, mutated, insane, randomly friendly or hostile each meeting. "Bingyanger" and "Support Unit" are placeholder joke names; Greg writes the real facility lore later.

## VICombat

Six **disciplines** live on the chip. They are playstyles, not weapon classes; any weapon works in any of them. You pick two on the brain screen after the breakout and unlock the rest during the escape, each with a chip-waking moment.

 | Discipline | Feel |

 | Duelist | Soulslike: stamina, lock-on, roll, parry and riposte |

 | Brawler | Mordhau and Half Sword: free directional swings, weight, grapples |

 | Berserker | Doom: speed, aggression, momentum, health from kills |

 | Shade | Stealth: takedowns from behind, darkness and light, hacking cameras and doors; also throwables, traps and improvised weapons |

 | Chaos Wizard | Sigil casting paid in blood or sanity, casting through organs marked in VATS, summoning and binding (Hornee is a summon) |

 | Marksman | Slow-time X-ray targeting, long range, Sniper Elite kills |

- **Controls:** Souls base everywhere (R1 light, R2 heavy, L1 block, L2 parry; with a gun R2 fires, L2 aims). Each discipline remaps its own layer, and on-screen hints follow it. Swapping costs chip heat; overheat glitches you.

- **Swap moment:** screen tear and CRT static, the HUD brain pulses, a modem chirp and a heartbeat.

- **Guns:** aim down the barrel, click the stick to zoom further. Bloodborne-style off-hand gun that staggers a windup. One-hand, two-hand and dual grips. Rib-cage weapon wheel around the HUD spine.

- **VATS:** squeeze L2 and R2 to slow time and pick parts. Arms disarm, legs, lungs and spine slow, eyes and head blind, ears deafen, jaw silences, hands fumble, spine hardware staggers.

- **Growth:** blood XP from kills, damage, your own blood and finishers. It feeds the brain board: one lobe per discipline, shared chip nodes in the middle, locked nodes visible but unreadable until you're near.

- **How you fight decides what survives:** overkill destroys the organs and cybernetics you'd loot; clean kills keep them.

- **Enemies use disciplines too** and are hard but fair: three to four hits, wounds that change how you fight, readable tells.

## VIIThe world

#### The Black Mirror

A stolen prototype phone with a jester on the back, and every route gives it to you. MAP, INDEX, WIRE, RADIO, CARRY, RITUAL, FIELD, camera with night vision, depth and photo mode. It's also a light, and light is what hunters see first.

#### The Board

No quest log. The story is a conspiracy wall you string together. Job posters with tear-off tabs and business cards from people feed it.

#### The Wire

The in-game internet: neocities-grade sites, personas with avatars, DMs, stalking a feed to find someone, a darkweb, a skin market. It's where the world's two records fight.

#### Money and the law

One currency with an issuer, bodies worth something by the part, liens and the bank. Witnesses carry what they saw, with a delay, to the faction that holds the ground.

#### Time and weather

One world clock that everything reads. A calendar of Ashfall, Emergence and Reaping, twelve thirty-day months and five Uncounted Days (Fool, Wound, Mirror, Wire, Flame). Rain, storms, acid rain.

#### Destruction

As close to Teardown as possible: walls and buildings are chunky voxels that break where you hit them, and the ground is smooth and diggable, so you can tunnel down. A five-tier toughness scale (soft, medium, hard, armoured, sealed) decides what breaks what: melee beats 1–2, guns 1–3, explosives 1–4, special charges, drills, torches, rams and high-altitude magick reach the top. That's how you blast into hidden underground cities and elite bunkers. Damage is recorded and repaired over a month by whoever holds the ground.

#### Random events

Every one to two minutes, GTA-style: a crazed driver, monks casting spirit visions. Any can turn into a quest, a trade or a boss.

#### Also in the world

The derby and its tunnels (a secret route), the club and its substances, CS-style cases with gold at 0.01%, local-AI NPCs you talk to by voice, Hornee, and the art gallery of Greg's work.

- The world keeps two records, and never arbitrates

- Time is real, and everything reads one clock

- Light is the resource

- The blow is performed, not requested

- A round is an object that travels and leaves brass

- Everything breaks, and the world keeps the damage

- The tutorial is a place, not a help screen

- Two charts, one Board: the pyramid and the tree

- Sigilisation is a real procedure

- The gods are what is actually worshipped

- The Godhead accumulates; it summons you

- The universe restarts and you do not

- Every screen is an object in the world

- Sound is a property of the world

- The psychedelic layer is a real pipeline
