## 1. REQUIREMENTS

### Game structure and progression

- **2026-09-19 — Persistent authored game, not runs.** Normal saves, a main-story end goal, post-game, and indefinite continued play. Quote: “It's not a roguelike.”
- **2026-09-19 — The first build boundary is not an in-fiction ending.** Build every intended escape route through arrival in the existing surface world, then continue development there. Quote: “we need to just do all those routes”.
- **2026-09-19 — Tutorialization must be physical and gradual.** The opening teaches UI, body inventory, access, weapons, anatomy and phone through actions rather than a list. Quote: “this whole part of the game teaches everything”.

### Opening, examiner and character creation

- **2026-09-19 — Start in a bloodied vat examination, not a dark spawn.** The readable facility is surveilled, locked down, esoteric and Outlast-horrific. Quote: “I don't think spawning into darkness.”
- **2026-09-19 — The examiner studies and questions the restrained player.** A mouth tube prevents normal speech; the brain chip exposes answers. Quote: “he just like instantly knows your answers”.
- **2026-09-19 — The examiner is personally cruel but oddly sympathetic.** He is a very senior government/laboratory doctor visiting from elsewhere. Quote: “personally cruel, but strangely sympathetic.”
- **2026-09-19 — The examination is recorded without consent.** Streaming as entertainment was liked but not settled. Quote: “It's recorded.”
- **2026-09-19 — Creation choices obey the player, while the institution may misclassify their meaning.** Quote: “it should obey the player exactly”.
- **2026-09-19 — All four creation approaches belong in play:** preset/direct customization, randomized body, natal chart, and personality instrument. Quote: “they should all be playable in the first version.”
- **2026-09-19 — Direct body creation is the priority.** Height, width, face, physical origin/race-like choice, male/female/androgynous/intersex/no-sex possibilities, and genital presentation were requested. Quote: “the most important is direct customization of the body”.
- **2026-09-19 — Creation is embedded into story beats rather than exposed as one giant form.** Quote: “you're slowly, like, creating who you are”.
- **2026-09-19 — Physical origin is player-selected, then institutionally classified.** Quote: “the combined approach is a good idea.”
- **2026-09-19 — The player initially cannot defeat the interrogation by lying.** Later traits/implants may allow resistance. Quote: “you can't at the moment.”
- **2026-09-19 — Breakout is the player's own soul/inner demon overriding the government implant into chaos magic.** Quote: “it's the player's own soul”.
- **2026-09-19 — The breakout is bodily and violent.** The player pulls the mouth tube, breaks restraints/glass, and emerges naked and helpless. Quote: “Yes, naked and helpless.”
- **2026-09-19 — The doctor chase is urgent desire, not a forced timer.** Missing him remains valid and preserves the longer revenge pursuit. Quote: “you're still gonna be looking for him”.
- **2026-09-19 — An apparent doctor kill is not permanent.** Medical reconstruction/body replacement returns him with memory and scars without invalidating the victory. Quote: “they get, like, resurrected medically”.

### Facility route, access and exits

- **2026-09-19 — Early acquisitions form a teaching chain:** medical/restraint tool, humiliation clothing, biometric access, a guard firearm with scarce ammunition, then the Black Mirror prototype. Quote: “medical restraint tools are a really good idea.”
- **2026-09-19 — Biometric doors accept physical solutions.** Coerce a guard, drag a face/body, remove a finger/hand/head, or later spoof authorization with the rewritten implant. Quote: “Yeah, that's all good ideas.”
- **2026-09-19 — The first gun comes from a guard and has very limited ammunition.** Greg's direct confirmation of that proposed beat: “Yep.”
- **2026-09-19 — The Black Mirror is stolen from restricted/special technology storage as a rare prototype.** Quote: “you stumble across it while trying to escape.”
- **2026-09-19 — Surface arrival leads into the existing main world rather than ending the game.** Quote: “it's still the world that we've got now”.

### World, population, territories and map

- **2026-09-19 — World scale is bounded.** The playable region should be no larger than roughly Fallout 4. Quote: “it can't be an insanely big map”.
- **2026-09-19 — Ordinary local population target is roughly 5–20 fully simulated people, depending on density.** Include moving groups, traders/caravans and lone wanderers, with desolate stretches. Quote: “5 to 20, depending on where you are.”
- **2026-09-19 — Distant people retain identity, destination and relationships while expensive invisible detail sleeps.** They become full individuals smoothly when near. Quote: “that's a really great idea.”
- **2026-09-19 — Liberating a territory is a consequential political act.** Discover occupation through jobs/world information, locate facilities on the map, then support occupiers, demand release/fight, or organize residents. Quote: “that's not liberating them. You're giving into corruption”.
- **2026-09-19 — The player can personally govern territory, but doing so attracts faction attention and raids.** Quote: “it makes it a point of interest for other factions”.
- **2026-09-19 — Liberated settlements can be invaded as rare story events, simulation events, or player-provoked events.** Quote: “all of the above, honestly.”
- **2026-09-19 — Rebuilding combines player choice, inhabitants, resources and procedural change over time.** Quote: “Combination”.
- **2026-09-19 — Desolated territory eventually recovers, retaining scars for a while.** Quote: “Definitely it can eventually recover”.
- **2026-09-19 — Allied battles support commands, preparation, leadership and objectives.** Quote: “Definitely all of the above.”
- **2026-09-19 — Natal-chart astronomy should use real planetary positions and personalize which planetary gods favour the character without disadvantaging builds.** Quote: “it's more personalized”.
